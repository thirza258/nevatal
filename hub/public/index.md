# Nevatal — Independent AI Studio

Nevatal is an independent AI studio at https://nevatal.id building focused AI workspaces, grounded retrieval tools, learning platforms, developer utilities, and engineering projects.

## Core Products & Platforms

- **Nevatal Chat Workspace** (https://chat.nevatal.id): A 17-tool AI workspace for writing, translating, document analysis, image generation, summarization, and data processing. Operates on a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model supporting OpenAI, Google Gemini, and OpenRouter with zero required user subscriptions.
- **RAG & Grounded Retrieval**: Advanced retrieval augmented generation platforms, document analysis, and conversational intelligence.
- **Courses & Learning**: Hands-on AI courses, prompt engineering guides, and interactive developer tutorials at https://chat.nevatal.id/courses.
- **Developer Utilities & Open Source**: Open source tools, C2PA provenance utilities, WebMCP interfaces, and web services.
